## Exact construction and semantics

- body q n is AAA S (SA)^n S^q A^(q-1), in newest-first order.
  SSFreeSupply.alt false n expands to exactly n SA pairs; no ordering
  reversal or alternative P2 predicate is used.
- word treats q=0,1,2 separately. At q>=3 pairCount_equation proves
  n>=1 and n+2q+2=q*q; square_ge_twice_add_three supplies the Nat
  subtraction bound and pairCount_int proves the intended integer value.
- body_twice_moment gives 2M=2(q*q-2q-2-n); it is used with that exact
  n, not a mass/moment hypothesis. body_P2 is a derived arithmetic
  corollary. body_sharp_length checks the stated 2q^2-2q-1 length.
- The decisive new statement is proper_mass_one_prefix: every positive
  proper prefix with mass=1 has one of the three exact positions and the
  corresponding moment 1, -i-2, or -n-3. All are nonzero.
  body_minimal derives exclusion of every proper P2 prefix.
- proper_mass_one_positions_iff and alt_S_prefix_at_pair prove the reverse
  direction: each listed proper position really has mass one. The
  forward classification is not an over-approximation disguised as equality.
- Four take regions cover every j: AAA S, SA pairs, S run, A run.
  In the final A run mass one forces the complete length, contradicting
  the proper-prefix premise. The empty prefix is excluded by j>0.
- body_ssCount computes overlapping junctions. n>=1 is proved for the
  chosen family; it is not assumed about all q. terminal_S_A_length
  counts reverse/takeWhile up to a genuine S, so it proves maximality.
- The small cases have finite kernel proofs of all P2, SS and tail
  properties and range-all proper-prefix exclusion; j remains universally
  quantified after range membership is proved.
- word_properties combines independently established properties for every
  q; the two existence corollaries repackage that word. These W corollaries
  are not counted as independent research. The module's R classification
  and actual SS calculation justify the evidence row.
- Informal -> formal and formal -> informal have the same explicit words,
  exact all-q quantifiers, mass/moment, overlap count and maximum-run
  meaning. There is no length cutoff, hidden minimality assumption or
  reachability condition. No canonical/seeded orbit occurrence follows.
- The only import is OneSSMultiplicity: E-362/E-363, FirstP2EndingS and
  TerminalASSBound are not in the import closure. Construction/minimality
  therefore do not consume the upper bound.
- Kernel controls show the general formula at q<3 is not P2, q=3 has
  n=1,length11,t=2, q=2 has a different length11 word with t=1, and
  a wrong n=0 at q=3 has mass/SS/tail but nonzero moment.
  SSS contributes two SS pairs. q=3 proper mass-one positions are exactly
  [1,5,7] with moments [1,-3,-4].
- Frozen computation reproduces q=0..40 and both-direction classification
  at q=3..40. The initial classification probe was exploratory; the
  saved script is frozen before the reproducibility rerun. Old discovery
  and holdout are explicitly reused. No fresh holdout claim is made.
