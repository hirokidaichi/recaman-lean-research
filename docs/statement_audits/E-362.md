## Meaning and dependency audit

- `defect_pos_before_p2`: the hypothesis excludes every nonempty P2 prefix,
  including the complete word. Its conclusion expands to
  `0 < moment w - (w.length : Int) * (mass w - 1)`. This is an auxiliary
  derived inequality, not a hypothesis silently added to the main theorem.
- `minimal_p2_ends_S`: its quantifiers exactly match issue #74. The positive
  proper-prefix exclusion becomes the auxiliary no-P2 condition on the
  word without its last symbol. The last sign A would force predecessor
  mass zero and D to increase by one, contradicting full P2.
- `first_p2_prefix_ends_S`: existence of some nonempty P2 prefix is necessary
  and explicit. Its output supplies a positive bounded index, P2, absence
  of every earlier positive P2 index, and S termination of that same prefix.
  The conclusion cannot be proved just by repackaging the existential input.
- Informal -> formal: newest-first A/S words encode as true/false lists;
  `mass` and `moment` use the original definitions. Prefix positions match
  the paper's H_j/M_j exactly. Minimality and ceiling include the same ranges.
- Formal -> informal: `w=u++[false]` is precisely S termination; no cutoff
  or orbit condition narrows the intended all-word claim. The least-index
  output is the paper's first P2 rather than some later S-ended witness.
- Counterfactuals: AASASSA has ceiling two and P2 at 3 and 7, so dropping
  minimality must permit its A end. AAASSSASASA has only P2 at 11 and ends
  A, so raising the ceiling to three must fail. Empty/A/ASSA have no P2
  prefix and cannot witness the existence premise. Frozen literal logs check
  all of these.

- The formal proof replaces the paper's excursion enumeration with the
