# General first-level core: scope and remaining cost

This is a paper consequence/audit of OSR, not a separately counted theorem. Let an original past end at v with global minimum m, old A count<=p, and initial height at least m+b+1. Consider any future interval whose A starts have height<=m+b+1. Let t be the first old visit to m+b. Every earlier height is at least m+b+1, whereas every possible source for a queried future A has height<=m+b. Thus every eligible old source is at or after t. Preserve the WHOLE word from t onward.

Since H(t)-H(v)=b, the exact walk count gives v-t=2A[t,v)+b<=2p+b. Prepending one S to this retained core places its new initial clock one level above every possible queried source. Both directions of complete-source transport hold. The comparison old core has length at most2p+b+1; old depth and upper height need no cutoff. Arbitrarily many future A are allowed, provided the explicit target-height band is satisfied. The band's first-hit application is derived from the question being tested.

The subtlety is the old ledger. Original old free[0,v) must remain separately charged. An old A above the queried future band can lose an earlier source when a prefix is removed, so one may NOT identify the comparison core's old free with original old free without a separate proof. This is exactly why the49-profile consumer proves the0/1/2/2 old free counts independently. OSR's `original_free_ledger` avoids that false identification.

One can prepend more S to the comparison core without changing the queried future sources, because the new clocks are all above the source-height band. A head of at least max(p+1,3) also gives negative heights throughout the old core and the stated future band, hence positive original deficit for that comparison word. Nevertheless its total free may differ from the old ledger. This is NOT a complete arbitrary-history counter-normalizer or a free-cost cancellation. It must not be promoted to uniform M<=F_low, and the general old-free-to-p producer remains unknown.

The principal depth-general no-go this evening is therefore independently supported by G0's exact all-m counterfamily. The source-radius operation supplies a useful conditional source-preserving interface; the fixed-free49-root consumer stays local. No fixed-rank classification, Lean helper count or finite absence resets the generalization tracker.
