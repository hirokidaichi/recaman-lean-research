## Meaning and dependencies

- Newest-first true/false words use exactly the original mass, moment and
  overlapping SS count. A run of three S contributes two SS pairs.
- The decomposition contains an actual final S followed by t A's. It
  therefore excludes shorter arbitrary A suffixes. Every P2 word admits
  it, and reverse/takeWhile has length t. Equality of decompositions
  implies equality of t: both directions of the maximum-run meaning hold.
- The weak bound has P2 alone. The strong bound explicitly has positive t
  and exclusion of every positive proper P2 prefix, with no length cutoff.
  Nat q-1 is saturated: q=0,t=0 is permitted.
- The paper's suffix-sum identity is proved by list induction as a
  nonnegative-suffix moment inequality. The proof derives total moment
  >=mass=1 if t>q, rather than assuming moment positivity.
- The strict proof derives every prefix mass<=2 from suffix mass>=-1 and
  total mass=1. E-362 supplies S termination for the minimal word. A
  positive terminal A run contradicts that termination.
- Informal -> formal and formal -> informal therefore have the same
  words, quantifiers and dependencies. No reachability, periodicity,
  owner map, Hall, delta bound or Gate T6 is asserted.

  strictness at t=0; AASASSA forbids dropping minimality; ASSA and A
