## Scope, falsification and application

A means all additions before the gap, not just nonsupplied ones. The hypothesis n<b is explicit; the initial n=b case uses the older no-window-after-S-run lemma. Removing BlockWord admits SSAAA | A S with j1/A1. Removing S-only admits SA | A with j1/A1. The concrete terminal tail is supported by four actual prefixes AASAAA/ASAAA/SAAASAAA/SSAAAASAAA; complete all-start ledgers are stored in terminal-support.json. Giving one further nonsupplied A permits a next A and breaks the terminal conclusion.

This supplies finite next-A checks and one terminal ray for a future compressed certificate. It does not prove the general rank, the whole r4 classification, or H.
