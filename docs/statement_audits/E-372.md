## Meaning, controls, and limitations

An earlier S at s with the same phase as q would also have S at s+1.
The starts s,q,q+1 are distinct even when s=q-1. All lie inside the
explicit donor window. This is why integer ordering alone is insufficient
and why the same-stream and window-bound hypotheses matter.
